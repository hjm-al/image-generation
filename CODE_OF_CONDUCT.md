# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in this project to be useful, direct, and respectful.
Contributors and maintainers should critique code and behavior without attacking
people, and should make room for different levels of experience and different
technical backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific about technical problems and evidence.
- Assume good faith while asking for reproducible details.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, images,
  messages, or identity without permission.
- Accept correction and focus on what improves the project.
- Keep reviews about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing private information or confidential material without consent.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include personal
or sensitive details in a public issue. If no private channel is available, ask
the maintainer to establish one before sharing the report.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may edit or remove content,
close a contribution, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. They will try to protect the reporter's privacy while gathering
enough context to make a fair decision.

This policy is informed by Contributor Covenant 2.1 and is intentionally concise
for this project.
